This Privacy Policy explains what information DU4 Music Publishing ("DU4", "we", "us") collects through the DU4 Music Publishing publishing tool (the "App"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What the App is

The App is an in-house tool that DU4 runs on its own computers. It prepares short video clips made from music and footage that DU4 owns or has licensed, and sends them to social media accounts that DU4 owns, through the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. On TikTok, the App only uploads clips to the account's inbox as drafts; a person then adds the sound, writes the caption and posts the clip in the TikTok app. The App is not offered to the public, and it does not read, collect or post anything for other TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube users.

2. Information we collect

When an account owner signs in to the App with TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or Google (YouTube) and grants permission, we receive and store:

  • Account identifiers: the platform's ID for the account (for example the TikTok open_id, the Facebook Page ID, the Instagram account ID or the YouTube channel ID) and its display name or channel name.
  • Access tokens: the sign-in tokens the platform issues so the App can upload on the account's behalf, and the permissions (scopes) that were granted.
  • Post records: for each clip we upload, the platform, the account, the time, the platform's post or upload ID, the link to the post once it is published, and later its view count, so we can track our own content and stay within each platform's daily limits.

From TikTok we request only the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. We do not collect passwords, contacts, messages, followers' information, location, payment information or any data about other users.

3. How we use information

  • To confirm that uploads go to the correct account that we own.
  • To upload our own clips to that account (as drafts on TikTok) after a person on our team has approved each upload.
  • To keep a record of what was posted, where and when, and to stay within platform limits.

We do not use the information for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, except as required by law.

4. Where information is stored and how it is protected

Access tokens are stored only on DU4's own computer, in a folder that only the computer's owner account can read. They are never written to our code repository, logs or reports. Post records are stored in DU4's private database. We use reasonable technical measures to protect this information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. How long we keep information

We keep access tokens until they expire, until access is revoked, or until they are no longer needed, whichever comes first. We keep post records for as long as we need them to manage our content and royalties, or as required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time in the platform's settings: TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions), Facebook and Instagram (Settings > Apps and websites / Business integrations), and Google (your Google Account > Security > Third-party apps with account access). Once access is revoked, the App can no longer upload.
  • Ask us to delete information we hold about an account by emailing info@du4music.com. We will delete it unless we must keep it by law.

7. Third-party platforms

When content is uploaded or posted on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube, the platform's own terms and privacy policy apply to how it handles that content and account. We are not responsible for those platforms' practices.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children and is only used by DU4's own team. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in their country).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If a change is significant, we will post a notice on this page.
